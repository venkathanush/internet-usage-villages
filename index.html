About the Project

This project aims to assess internet usage patterns in Kovur village and provide digital awareness to bridge the connectivity gap. With the rapid digital transformation, understanding internet accessibility and usage in rural areas is crucial for inclusive development.

The project was conducted over 8 weeks (1 June 2026 to 26 July 2026), consisting of a 6-week main project and a 2-week mini project focused on internet awareness and recommendations.
